BASIC PLAYER ANIMATIONS

Open Assets/Scenes/Maged.unity and press Play.
The player starts frozen for 3 seconds, then you can move.

Controls
- WASD: move; Shift: run; hold C: crouch; Space: jump.
- Hold right mouse: aim (walking is allowed, running is disabled).
- Hold E while standing still: recharge. Release E or move to cancel.
- Escape: pause/resume.

Where things are
- Player still contains the existing movement, camera and state scripts.
- Player > Character Visual contains the body and its active Animator.
- Both PlayerMovement and PlayerStateManager reference that Animator.
- The original empty Animator on Player is disabled.
- AC_FirstPersonPlayer contains one layer and six simple states.
- Locomotion, Run and Crouch contain directional blend trees.
- Root motion is off: CharacterController still moves the player.

Seeing the character
The camera script enables the character meshes at startup instead of hiding them.
The camera is still first-person: look down to see the body, or watch it in
Scene view during Play to see the whole character. The camera is at head
height, so the model may clip into view; no separate first-person mesh is used.
Beta_Joints and Beta_Surface are mesh parts included in the Mixamo character.

Temporary animation substitutes
- Forward walk: Rifle Run at 0.55 playback speed.
- Backward/sideways run: the available walking clips.
- Crouch idle: Rifle Kneel Idle (a kneeling pose, not a proper crouch idle).
- Crouch sideways: crouch forward clip (feet will not match sideways motion).
- Jump/fall: Rifle Idle until a jump/fall clip is supplied.
- Recharge: Rifle Idle until a recharge clip is supplied.
- Aim uses the existing rifle poses; there is no separate aim animation,
  weapon model or upper-body layer. PlayerCombat zooms the camera while aiming.
- The character uses a simple grey-blue URP material.

Replacing a substitute
Import a suitable Humanoid animation. Open AC_FirstPersonPlayer in Animator.
Double-click the appropriate blend tree, then replace its Motion field.
For forward walking, also change the new clip's playback speed from 0.55 to 1.
For jump/recharge, select the placeholder state and replace its Motion field.

This is the basic LOCAL animation setup. Network synchronization, remote
player ownership and weapon attachment are not implemented here.
